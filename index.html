1 Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use ForFood:

a) Information You Provide Directly

  • Account details: full name, email address, and password (encrypted).
  • Optional profile data: phone number and profile photo.
  • Delivery addresses: labels, full addresses, and pinned map coordinates you save in your address book.
  • Order details: items ordered, quantities, order status, and total amount.
  • Chat messages: messages exchanged with a restaurant about a specific order.
  • Reviews: star ratings and comments you leave for restaurants.
  • Restaurant owners: restaurant name, address, geolocation, description, menu items with photos and prices, and optional phone number.

b) Information Collected Automatically

  • Location data: precise GPS coordinates (with your permission) to show nearby restaurants and calculate delivery distance.
  • Device tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications.
  • Usage data: restaurant views, order counts, and in-app interactions used to power recommendations and "High Demand" sections.

c) Information from Third Parties

  • Google Sign-In: if you choose Google login, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Create and manage your account, and authenticate you securely.
  • Process orders, send them to restaurants, and track their status.
  • Calculate distances and show restaurants, dishes, and prices near you.
  • Enable real-time chat between you and a restaurant for a specific order.
  • Send push notifications about order status (accepted, preparing, ready, delivered).
  • Display your saved delivery addresses for faster checkout.
  • Publish customer reviews and compute restaurant ratings.
  • Manage restaurant subscriptions (via RevenueCat).
  • Improve the app, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3 Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on your jurisdiction, we process your data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the Service you signed up for (accounts, orders, chat, reviews).
  • Consent: for location access, push notifications, camera / photo access — which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests: to prevent fraud, improve the Service, and ensure security.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws and valid requests from public authorities.

4 Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in these cases:

  • With restaurants: when you place an order, we share your name, phone number, email, delivery address, order details, and any chat messages to fulfill the order.
  • With service providers (listed in Section 5) who process data strictly on our behalf.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights and safety of our users, our team, or the public.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred, and we will notify you.

6 Location Data

ForFood requests access to your device's precise location so we can show restaurants near you and calculate delivery distance. Location access is granted only while you use the app and can be revoked at any time in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ForFood
  • Android: Settings → Apps → ForFood → Permissions → Location

If you deny location access, you can still use the app by manually picking a location on the map.

7 Camera & Photo Library

With your permission, ForFood accesses your photo library so you can upload a profile picture (users) or menu item photos and gallery images (restaurant owners). We do not access the camera or microphone during normal operation.

8 Push Notifications

We send push notifications about order status, promotions, and account activity. You can disable these in your device settings at any time. We store an FCM device token to deliver notifications; it's deleted when you log out.

9 Data Retention

  • Account data: kept while your account is active.
  • Order history: kept for up to 3 years for tax, accounting, and dispute purposes.
  • Chat messages: kept while the order exists in your history.
  • Location: used in real time only — we do not store a location history.

When you delete your account, we erase personal data within 30 days, except data we are legally required to keep (e.g., financial records).

10 Data Security

We protect your data using industry-standard measures:

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firebase Firestore security rules restrict access to your own data.
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

No system is 100% secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

11 Children's Privacy

ForFood is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us at ForFoodSupport@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

12 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (editable in-app via Profile).
  • Delete your account and data ("Delete Account" in Settings).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Portability — receive a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent for location, notifications, or photo access at any time.

To exercise these rights, email ForFoodSupport@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

13 International Transfers

ForFood uses Google Firebase servers, which may store and process your data in countries outside your own (including the United States). We rely on standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards to protect your data during these transfers.

14 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page and, for material changes, notify you in the app. Continued use of ForFood after changes means you accept the updated policy.

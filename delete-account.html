Delete Your ForFood Account

ForFood lets you delete your account and personal data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

In the app (recommended):
  1. Open the ForFood app and log in.
  2. Tap the profile icon (top-right) to open the side menu.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm by tapping Yes, delete.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, password)
  • Your profile information (name, phone number, profile photo)
  • Your saved delivery addresses
  • Your notifications
  • Your chat messages and account-linked content

What is retained

  • Order records are retained in anonymized form for up to 3 years for tax, accounting, and legal-compliance purposes. Your name, email, phone, and delivery address are removed from these records immediately.
  • Aggregated ratings data may be retained for restaurant rating calculations.

Data deletion timeline

Personal data is deleted within 30 days of your request, except where we are legally required to retain it.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email ForFoodSupport@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account" and the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.
